* Introduction

It is important to keep learning, but at different stages of life, there are different things to focus on. Right now, emacs lisps is not something I am ready to dive deep into. I went back to using ~use-package~ to simplify the configuration process with better result.

Many settings are still documented, but not to the extend of before. Ring me up for detail.

      (treesit-install-language-grammar (car grammar))))
